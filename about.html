Why we exist

Five vendors, five invoices, and nothing that talks to each other.

A growing business ends up with a web agency, a marketing agency, a freelancer for the app, an accountant's recommendation for software, and somebody's cousin managing the ads. Each one does their part. Nobody owns the whole. So the owner becomes the integration layer — re-typing orders, chasing leads between tools, and reconciling numbers that should have matched automatically.

That's not a technology problem. It's an ownership problem. We take the whole thing.

How we work

Slow on scope. Fast on build.

Most projects fail in the first week, not the last. We spend the time up front getting the scope exactly right, then build fast against it.

  • 01 Discovery call — 30 minutes. What the business does, where the hours go, what's actually broken.
  • 02 Systems audit — We map your current tools and where data stops moving between them. You keep the map either way.
  • 03 Blueprint — Fixed scope, fixed price, defined milestones, in writing before any money moves.
  • 04 Design — Wireframes and UI you sign off on before a line of code is written.
  • 05 Build — Two-week cycles. Working software at the end of each, not a status update.
  • 06 Test and launch — Cross-browser, cross-device, performance and security checks before going live.
  • 07 Handover and run — Documentation, training, and everything registered in your name.
