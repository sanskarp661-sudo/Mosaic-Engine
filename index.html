What does a project cost?

Websites start at ₹85,000. ERPNext implementations start at ₹1,25,000. Automation builds start at ₹65,000. Marketing retainers start at ₹25,000 a month plus ad spend. We quote a fixed price after the audit.

Two to six weeks depending on scale, page count and integrations. You see a working version at the two-week mark either way.

Yes. Custom ERP modules, CRM dashboards and automated data pipelines built around how your business already works.
