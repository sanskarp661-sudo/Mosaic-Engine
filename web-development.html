The problem

A website that looks fine and does nothing.

Most business websites are brochures. They load slowly, they read like a company profile, and they give a visitor no obvious next step. Meanwhile the enquiries that do come in land in an inbox nobody checks and never reach the CRM. The site isn't broken. It just isn't connected to anything that makes money.

What we build

What you get.

  • Ecommerce storefronts — catalogue, payments, order management, wired to your inventory
  • Landing pages — single-purpose pages built for campaigns, with tracking that works
  • Brand and UI design — visual identity, design system, component library
  • Technical foundation — performance budgets, structured data, semantic markup, accessibility

How it connects

A site is the front door, not the building.

Your website is where a customer first meets you, but on its own it's a dead end. We wire it into what happens next: the enquiry form writes straight into your ERP, campaign traffic carries its source through to the sales team via attribution tracking, and after-hours enquiries get answered by AI on WhatsApp instead of waiting until Monday. If you need a customer-facing product beyond the browser, the same data drives the mobile app.

Questions

How long does a website take?

Two to six weeks depending on page count and integrations. You see a working version at the two-week mark regardless.

Do I own the code?

Yes. Repository, hosting, domain and every account are registered in your name from day one.

Can you redesign my existing site?

Yes. We audit where the current site loses visitors, modernise the stack, and rebuild around the actions that matter. We keep whatever's working.

Will it work on mobile?

Every build is mobile-first. Most Indian traffic is mobile, so the phone layout is designed before the desktop one.

Do you do SEO as well?

The technical foundation is built in — speed, structured data, clean markup. Ongoing content and ranking work is digital marketing, quoted separately.

What happens after launch?

Handover, documentation and training are included. Ongoing maintenance is optional, not compulsory.
