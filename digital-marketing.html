The problem

The agency reports impressions, clicks and cost-per-lead. Nobody reports revenue. Leads land in a form inbox, get copied into a spreadsheet, and lose their source on the way. Three months later you're deciding next quarter's budget on a number that only measures the top of the funnel.

Questions

Do you require a long contract?

No. Month to month after the first three, which is the minimum for campaign data to be meaningful.

Is ad spend included in your fee?

No. Ad spend goes directly to Google or Meta from accounts in your name. We're paid for management only, so we have no incentive to inflate budgets.

How soon will I see results?

Paid campaigns produce data in days and stabilise in four to six weeks. SEO is three to six months before meaningful movement. Anyone promising faster on SEO is guessing.

Do I own the ad accounts?

Yes. Google Ads, Meta Business Manager, GA4 and Search Console all created in your name, with us added as a user. If you leave, you keep the history.

Can you work with our existing website?

Yes, though we'll audit it first. Campaign spend against a slow or unclear site wastes money, and we'll tell you if that's the bottleneck before taking the retainer.
