The problem

Most business apps are a website in a box.

An app earns its place only when it does something the browser can't — remembers the customer, notifies them, works when the signal drops, sits on the home screen. Apps that just wrap the website get installed once and deleted. And an app that doesn't read from the same data as your team creates a second version of the truth to reconcile.

What we build

What you get.

  • Customer apps — bookings, orders, accounts, loyalty, push notifications
  • Client portals — a logged-in space for customers to track their own orders or projects
  • Internal tools — field staff apps, inventory scanning, approval workflows
  • Progressive web apps — installable, offline-capable, no app store approval needed
  • Integration — every screen reads from the same data your ERP and website use

How it connects

Same data, different screen.

The app isn't a separate system. It reads from the same ERP your team works in, so an order placed in the app appears in inventory instantly and a status change in the back office reaches the customer's phone without anyone updating two places. It shares its design system with your website, and in-app enquiries route through the same automated qualification as every other channel.

Questions

Android and iOS both?

Yes. We build in React Native, so one codebase covers both platforms.

Do I need an app at all?

Often not. If your customers visit occasionally, a fast mobile site is cheaper and works better. We'll say so on the call rather than sell you a build you don't need.

How long does it take?

Six to twelve weeks for a full app, depending on scope. Progressive web apps are faster because there's no store approval.

Do you handle App Store and Play Store submission?

Yes, including the developer accounts, which are registered in your name.

What about ongoing updates?

Both stores require periodic updates for OS compatibility. We offer a maintenance plan, or hand over cleanly if you'd rather do it in-house.
