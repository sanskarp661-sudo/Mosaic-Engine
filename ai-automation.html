Questions

Is WhatsApp automation allowed?

Yes, through the official WhatsApp Cloud API. We don't use unofficial tools or automation bots that risk your number being banned.

Will customers know they're talking to AI?

Yes, and it should say so. Disclosure builds more trust than pretending, and customers mostly care about a fast, correct answer.

What if the AI gets something wrong?

It hands off to a human at defined thresholds — anything about pricing, complaints, or anything it isn't confident about. You set the boundaries.

Do I need an ERP first?

No, but automation is worth far more with one. Without a central system the AI has nowhere consistent to write, and you end up with a fast process feeding a messy one.

What does it cost to run?

WhatsApp conversation charges from Meta and AI API usage, both billed to your accounts. For most small businesses this is a small monthly figure, and we'll estimate it before you commit.
